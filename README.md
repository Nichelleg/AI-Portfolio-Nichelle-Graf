I am a returning adult student pursuing a BAT in Applied AI & Robotics, bringing over a decade of experience in real estate and property management into the AI space. My focus is on building practical, production-oriented AI systems — particularly at the intersection of NLP, cybersecurity, and intelligent automation.
My first degree is a B.L.S. in Communications & Media Studies from the University of Iowa — which turns out to be relevant in AI systems that process and understand human language.
Before returning to school, I spent 10+ years in real estate investment and Airbnb property management. That background shapes how I approach AI: I think about systems that have to work in the real world, under real constraints, with real consequences for getting it wrong.

## Featured Projects

### NewsBot Intelligence System
*ITAI 2373 — NLP Midterm & Final Project*

A production-ready news categorization and querying platform built end-to-end across two project phases. The midterm delivered a multi-pipeline NLP system using TF-IDF and topic modeling (LDA/NMF) for automated news classification. The final project (NewsBot 2.0) extended it with transformer-based embeddings, named entity recognition, and a conversational AI interface for live news querying.

→ [View Repository](https://github.com/Nichelleg/NLP-ITAI2373)

### SLOP STOP — RAG-Based Content Integrity API
*ITAI 2371 — AI in Cybersecurity Midterm*

A startup-pitched API for detecting AI-generated content using a Retrieval-Augmented Generation pipeline. Built with Gemini 2.5 Flash, FAISS vector store, and sentence-transformers. Designed to serve content platforms, academic institutions, and media organizations needing verifiable content authenticity.
